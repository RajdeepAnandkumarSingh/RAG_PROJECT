# Flipkart Support Return & Replacement Policy

## 7-Day Replacement Guarantee
Flipkart Support provides a **7-day replacement guarantee** from the date of delivery for eligible electronics, smartwatches, audio devices, and mobile accessories.

- **Replacement Eligibility**: Defective, damaged, or mismatched items reported within 7 calendar days are eligible for an instant replacement or pickup via **Ekart Logistics**.
- **Open-Box Inspection**: High-value electronics delivered via Ekart Logistics may include Open-Box Delivery verification at the doorstep.
- **Refund Timeline**: When a replacement unit is out of stock, refunds are processed to the original payment method or Flipkart Gift Card within **5 to 7 business days** after Ekart pickup.
- **Order Tracking**: Live delivery updates for Flipkart orders are tracked through Ekart Logistics.
