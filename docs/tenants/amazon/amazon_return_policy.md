# Amazon Support Return & Refund Policy

## 30-Day Electronics Return Window
Amazon Support provides a **30-day electronics return window** from the date of delivery for eligible consumer electronics, smart home devices (including Echo and Fire hardware), laptops, and accessories.

- **Condition Requirements**: Returned electronics must include all original accessories, power adapters, and manufacturer packaging.
- **Amazon Prime Logistics Pickup**: Prime-eligible returns qualify for free doorstep pickup or drop-off at Amazon Hub Locker locations.
- **Refund Timeline**: Refunds are credited back to the original payment method within **3 to 5 business days** after carrier scan or inspection.
- **Order Tracking**: Live shipment updates for Amazon orders are managed through Amazon Prime Logistics.
