# Nexus Enterprise Customer Support Knowledge Base & Policies

## Return and Refund Policy
- Items purchased within the last 30 days are eligible for a full refund or exchange.
- To qualify for a return, the merchandise must be in its original packaging, unused, and include all accessories, manuals, and receipts.
- Digital products, downloadable software, and gift cards are strictly non-refundable once delivered.
- Return shipping is free for all defective items or incorrect orders shipped by Nexus. For standard buyer remorse returns, a flat return shipping fee of $5.99 is deducted from the refund.
- Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days after our warehouse inspects the returned package.

## Shipping and Order Delivery Policy
- Standard Shipping: Delivered within 3-5 business days across the contiguous United States.
- Express Priority Shipping: Delivered within 1-2 business days with priority courier tracking.
- Order Tracking: Live tracking information with tracking number and carrier status is generated within 24 hours of dispatch.
- Address Changes: Shipping addresses can be modified only within 1 hour of placing the order before dispatch.

## Warranty and Hardware Replacement
- All Nexus hardware products include a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty covering hardware defects in materials and workmanship under normal consumer use.
- The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid ingress, unauthorized third-party modifications, or normal cosmetic wear and tear.
- To submit a warranty claim, provide your original order invoice and device serial number to technical support.
- If a hardware defect is verified, Nexus provides a certified replacement device or repair service at no charge.

## Billing, Disputes, and Cancellation
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge if the cancellation request is initiated before shipment dispatch.
- If your account shows duplicate or erroneous charges, contact billing support immediately for an expedited reversal within 24-48 business hours.
- For unresolved billing disputes or manager escalations, requests are routed directly to Senior Billing Specialists for manual review.
