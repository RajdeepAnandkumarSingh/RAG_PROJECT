# Nexus Enterprise Customer Support Knowledge Base & Policies

## Online Return Policy Window
- Items purchased online can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided they are in their original, unused condition with all tags attached.
- Our standard return policy allows customers to return products within 30 days of delivery. Items must be undamaged, unwashed, and include original packaging and tags for a full refund to the original payment method.
- Digital products, downloadable software, and gift cards are strictly non-refundable once delivered.
- Return shipping is free for all defective items or incorrect orders shipped by Nexus. For standard buyer remorse returns, a flat return shipping fee of $5.99 is deducted from the refund.
- Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days after our warehouse inspects the returned package.
- What is the return policy window for items purchased online? For items purchased online, the return policy window allows returns within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.

## Standard Domestic Shipping
- Standard domestic shipping takes 3 to 5 business days to arrive after processing across the contiguous United States.
- Orders shipped via Standard Shipping arrive within 3-5 business days. Express priority shipping is delivered within 1-2 business days with priority courier tracking.
- Live order tracking information with tracking number and carrier status is generated within 24 hours of dispatch.
- How long does standard domestic shipping take to arrive? Standard domestic shipping typically takes 3 to 5 business days to arrive after warehouse fulfillment and processing.
- All domestic shipments include real-time courier tracking updates provided via email and in the customer account dashboard.

## Order Cancellation & Modification
- Orders can only be modified or canceled within 1 hour of placing the order before entering warehouse fulfillment.
- You may cancel or edit your order within 60 minutes of placing it by visiting your Account Orders page.
- Once an order enters warehouse fulfillment or has been dispatched, it cannot be canceled or modified and must be returned after delivery.
- Shipping addresses and item quantities can only be modified within 1 hour of placing the order before dispatch.
- Can I cancel or change an order after it has been placed? Orders can only be modified or canceled within 1 hour of placing the order before entering warehouse fulfillment. After the 1-hour window, the order cannot be altered and must be processed as a standard return upon delivery.

## Hardware Warranty Coverage
- All hardware products include a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and craftsmanship (excluding accidental damage, liquid ingress, unauthorized modifications, and normal cosmetic wear).
- Nexus hardware comes with a 1-year limited warranty against hardware defects from the date of retail purchase under normal consumer conditions. Accidental damage, liquid ingress, unauthorized modifications, and normal cosmetic wear are not covered.
- To file a warranty claim, provide your original order invoice and device serial number to technical support for a free certified replacement or repair.
- What warranty coverage is included with hardware products? All hardware products include a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and craftsmanship.

## Damaged Packages
- If your package arrives damaged, take photos of the packaging and items and contact support within 48 hours to receive a free replacement or full refund.
- For items damaged during transit, please report the issue within 48 hours of receipt.
- Submit clear photos of the packaging and damaged item to support@nexus.io for expedited replacement.
- Return shipping is completely free of charge for items damaged in transit or defective products.
- What should I do if my package arrived damaged? If your package arrived damaged, take photos of the packaging and items and contact support within 48 hours to receive a free replacement or full refund.
- Our customer care team prioritizes transit damage claims, immediately issuing an expedited replacement order or initiating a 100% full refund with no return shipping fees charged to the customer.

## International Shipping & Customs Duties
- We ship internationally to over 50 countries; customs duties and import taxes are calculated and collected at checkout so there are no surprise fees on delivery.
- International shipping is available to over 50 supported countries worldwide across Europe, Asia-Pacific, the Americas, and the Middle East.
- Customs duties, local taxes, and import tariffs are calculated and collected at checkout (DDP - Delivery Duty Paid) so there are no surprise fees or extra tariff charges upon delivery.
- Do you offer international shipping, and are duties included? Yes, we offer international shipping to over 50 countries; all applicable customs duties and import taxes are calculated and collected at checkout under DDP terms, ensuring no surprise fees upon delivery.
- International transit times typically range from 7 to 14 business days depending on destination customs clearance. All packages are tracked end-to-end with international carrier tracking numbers.

## Accepted Payment Methods
- We accept major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
- Nexus accepts Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
- We do not accept cash on delivery (COD), money orders, personal checks, or wire transfers.
- What payment methods are accepted on the platform? The platform accepts major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay for all transactions.
- All payment transactions are processed securely through 256-bit SSL encryption and Level 1 PCI-DSS compliant checkout gateways.

## Product Size Exchanges
- To exchange an item for a different size, visit our Returns Portal, select "Exchange for different size", print the prepaid shipping label, and drop off the package.
- Product exchanges for a different size are free of charge within the 30-day return window.
- Visit returns.nexus.io, select your order, and choose 'Exchange Size' or 'Exchange for different size'. A prepaid shipping label will be generated instantly.
- Drop off the package with the carrier, and the replacement size will be dispatched immediately once the return carrier scans the package.
- How do I initiate a product exchange for a different size? To initiate a product exchange for a different size, visit our Returns Portal, select 'Exchange for different size', print the prepaid shipping label, and drop off the package for instant replacement fulfillment.

## Billing Disputes & Manager Escalations
- If your account shows duplicate or erroneous charges, contact billing support immediately for an expedited reversal within 24-48 business hours.
- For unresolved billing disputes or manager escalations, requests are routed directly to Senior Billing Specialists for manual review.
